لمّة · Lamma
«لمّها في مشوار واحد»
Gather it up, in one trip. Alt: «عيش ودوا وخضار.. في مشوار واحد»

A gathering: «لمّة العيلة», «لمّة رمضان». And the verb Egyptians already use, «لمّها», gather it up: three shops into one trip, the one thing a phone call can't do. Riders are «سبع» («تسلم يا سبع»).

Risks: «اتلمّ!» is a scolding; an Egyptian social app «Lamma - لمة» already exists; in Latin always LAMMA (double M), never Lama or Llama. Never show the shadda alone: Latin readers see a "W".

The shadda «ّ» has three prongs in one stroke: three shops, one trip. And «شدّة» means strength. It is redrawn as its own lantern-yellow mark, the only lit thing on a Ramadan-night tile.

How Lamma uses yellow without joining the yellow cluster: yellow only does jobs that need light (the rider bag at night, the shop-door sticker, the main button). The app tile is Ramadan-night navy with one lantern-yellow shadda, so on the home screen Lamma is the only dark tile with a lit mark, not a fourth yellow square next to Fawry, noon and Rabbito.
