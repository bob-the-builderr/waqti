Primary lockup · on Volt Violet
واو · WAW · v2, the founder's pick
«متنزلش.. واو يوصّلك»
Alt: «عيش و دوا و خضار.. في مشوار واحد.»

The place-order button says «وصّلي» ("deliver it to me"). It is a common delivery word, and an Egyptian app named Wasally exists, so «وصّلي» is only ever a button and slogan word. It is never registered as our mark; the mark is «واو».

Rule: never draw the loop as a plain circle on a short vertical tail, because that is ٩. The road always runs flat to the left and ends at the door.

The و is the trip: its loop runs through three shops (three mint stations, like a metro map), and its tail is the road that ends at your door (the terminus).

Confident, not cute: transit-map geometry, heavy strokes, caps Latin, no mascot and no bubbles. Explored and dropped: three stops bunched on top (read as a paw print), an arched door (read as a tombstone), a kufic square waw (strong but generic). See iterations/.
