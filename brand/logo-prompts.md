# Logo prompts — Adham (أدهم)

Concept: a black horse silhouette with no inner details, carrying a motorcycle delivery top box on its back.
Palette: black #141414, signal yellow #FFC72C, cream #FAF6EE.
Leave the name out of every prompt; the Arabic wordmark is set afterwards in Alexandria.
Avoid rearing or prancing poses (too close to Lloyds Bank, Ferrari, Porsche, Ford Mustang).

## 1. Head and neck + box (closest to the current sketch)

```
Minimal flat vector logo mark for a delivery app. A solid black silhouette of a horse's head and neck in profile, facing left, with no inner details at all: no eye, no mane strands, no nostrils, just one clean bold shape with smooth confident curves. Strapped on the horse's back, just behind its neck, sits a motorcycle delivery top box drawn as a simple cube in signal yellow #FFC72C with a thin black outline. Colors: black #141414 and signal yellow #FFC72C on a plain cream background #FAF6EE. Geometric, modern, timeless, conveys strength and speed. Must stay readable as a tiny 24-pixel app icon. No text, no letters, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D rendering, no mockup. Centered with generous empty margin.
```

## 2. Full galloping horse + box

```
Minimal flat vector logo symbol. A solid black silhouette of a horse in full gallop, side view, running to the left, legs stretched forward and back, no details inside the shape. A signal-yellow #FFC72C cube-shaped motorcycle delivery box is strapped on its back like a saddle. Bold, simple, dynamic, conveys speed and strength. Flat colors only: black #141414 and yellow #FFC72C on cream #FAF6EE. Not rearing, not prancing, all four legs in a running stride. No text, no gradients, no shadows, no outlines except around the box. Centered, logo design.
```

## 3. App icon

```
Mobile app icon, rounded square with a solid black #141414 background. In the center, a cream #FAF6EE silhouette of a horse's head and neck facing left, with a small signal-yellow #FFC72C cube delivery box on its back. Flat, bold, minimal, high contrast, readable at small sizes. No text, no gradients, no gloss, no 3D.
```

## 4. Negative space (bolder idea)

```
Clever minimal logo. A solid black #141414 silhouette of a horse's head and neck facing left, where a square delivery box is cut out of the neck as negative space and filled with signal yellow #FFC72C, so horse and box read as one unified shape. Flat vector, bold, geometric, no inner details, no text, plain cream #FAF6EE background, centered.
```

## Midjourney (short form)

Add after any prompt above, or use on its own:

```
minimal flat vector logo, solid black horse silhouette facing left, no inner details, yellow cube delivery box strapped on its back, black #141414 and yellow #FFC72C on cream, bold geometric, app icon friendly --ar 1:1 --style raw --no text, letters, gradient, shadow, 3d, realistic, mockup, rearing
```

## Recraft (gives an editable SVG)

Pick a Vector style (Icon or flat vector), set the palette to the three colors above, paste prompt 1 or 3, and export SVG.
