بشير · Basheer
«بشير خير.. طلبك جاي»
A good sign: your order is on its way.

The bringer of good news. In Surat Yusuf it is literally a courier: «فلما أن جاء البشير», the one who carried the shirt home. Egyptians say «بشير خير» for any good sign.

The icon is the letter ش from the middle of the name. Its three teeth are three stops on one stroke, and its three dots are three shops. That is the multi-shop trip, written in one letter. The dots glow in sun yellow on Fayoum brick, the colour of the Tunis-village pottery.
