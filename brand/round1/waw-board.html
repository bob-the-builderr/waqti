Primary lockup · on Waw Violet
واو · waw
«عيش ودوا وخضار.. في مشوار واحد»
Bread and medicine and veg, in one trip.

The letter و, "and". The one word you say three times when you order: «عيش و دوا و خضار». It is also the sound people make when it arrives early: واو. واو and waw are both palindromes, in Arabic and in Latin.

The icon is a calligraphic و: a small loop (the pickup loop around your shops) and a long tail that sweeps out to your door. The tail is drawn long and tapered so it reads as Arabic, never as a "9". The mint dot means "delivered" and appears only at the end of a trip.
